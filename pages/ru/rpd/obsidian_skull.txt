====== Obsidian Skull ======

{{ rpd:images:black_skull_item.png|Obsidian Skull }}

**Obsidian Skull** is a small obsidian skull item dropped from the [[ru:rpd:lich_mob|Lich]]. It was the source of the Necropolis's powers, but after the Lich's defeat it lost them — yet a strange thought remains: "It hungers".

==== Overview ====
  * **Type:** [[ru:rpd:items|Item]]
  * **Usage:** Related to the Necromancer quest and [[ru:rpd:lich_subclass|Lich subclass]]
  * **Drop:** [[ru:rpd:lich_mob|Lich]]

==== Acquisition ====
  * Dropped by [[ru:rpd:lich_mob|Lich]] in the Necropolis
  * Used in the [[ru:rpd:necromancer_class|Necromancer]] quest
