===== Mechanics =====
  * **Hunger levels**: 0 (full) to 400 (starving)
  * **Hungry threshold**: 280 - hero gets hungry debuff
  * **Starving threshold**: 400 - hero takes damage over time
  * **Food energy**: Restores hunger level (reduces hunger value)
  * **Mystery Meat energy**: STARVING - HUNGRY = 120 units
  * **Ration energy**: HUNGRY = 280 units
  * **Chargrilled Meat/Frozen Carpaccio energy**: STARVING - HUNGRY = 120 units
