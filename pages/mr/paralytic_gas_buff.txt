===== Sources & Effects =====
**Sources that create Paralytic Gas:**
  * **Potion of Paralytic Gas** (`PotionOfParalyticGas.java`) - Thrown potion creates paralytic gas cloud
  * **Paralytic Trap** (`ParalyticTrap.java`) - Trap creates paralytic gas when triggered
  * **Rotten Food** - Eating has chance to create paralytic gas

**Mechanics:**
  * Extends `Blob` class
  * In `evolve()`: applies Paralysis buff to characters standing in gas
  * Duration scaled by `CharUtils.durationFactor(ch)`
  * Visual effect: `Speck.PARALYTIC` particles
  * Characters with gas immunity are unaffected
