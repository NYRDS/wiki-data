===== Entity Properties =====
  * **Type**: Hero Class
  * **Magic Affinity**: Elemental
  * **Starting Spell Power**: 15 (maxSp and sp)
  * **Is Spell User**: true
  * **Starting Equipment**:
    - Wand of Magic Missile (identified, in quickslot)
    - Scroll of Identify (known from start)
  * **Starting Spell**: Wind Gust (in quickslot)
  * **Class Armor**: Mage Armor (Molten Earth ability)
  * **Special Abilities**:
    - Unique Wand of Magic Missile that can be disenchanted to upgrade other wands
    - Faster wand recharge rate
    - Food restores 1 charge to all wands in inventory
    - Can use wands as melee weapons
    - Uses mana to cast Elemental spells
    - Scrolls of Identify pre-identified

===== Class Armor Special Ability =====
  * **Ability Name**: Molten Earth
  * **Action String**: MageArmor_ACSpecial -> "MOLTEN EARTH"
  * **Effect**: Sets all visible enemies on fire and roots them in place
  * **Restriction**: Only mages can use this armor
