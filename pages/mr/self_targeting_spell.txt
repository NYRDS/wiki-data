===== Self-Targeting Behavior =====
When `targetingType = "self"`:
  * Spell is cast on the caster directly
  * No target selection UI is shown
  * `CustomSpell.cast(Char chr)` is called (the no-argument version)
  * Lua `cast(self, spell, caster)` function receives only the caster
  * Typical for buffs, self-buffs, and area effects centered on caster
