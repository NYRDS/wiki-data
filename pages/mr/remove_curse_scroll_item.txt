===== Additional Information =====
  * Scroll class: ScrollOfRemoveCurse extends Scroll
  * Price when identified: 30 gold per quantity
  * Removes curses from all equipped items
  * Removes curses from known cursed items in inventory
  * Dispels WEAKNESS buff from reader
  * Visual effect: Flare(6, 32) + ShadowParticle.UP emitter
  * Sound: SND_READ
  * Time to read: TIME_TO_READ constant
  * Static uncurse methods available for programmatic use (uncurse(Belongings), uncurse(Char, Item...))
