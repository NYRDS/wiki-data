===== Properties =====
  * **Entity kind:** "Anesthesia" (BuffFactory.ANESTHESIA)
  * **Icon:** 26 (in buff descriptor)
  * **Function:** Prevents waking from damage while sleeping; does not reduce damage taken
  * **Duration:** 10 × skill level turns (from Anesthesia spell)
  * **Interaction:** Works in conjunction with Sleep buff - Sleep puts target to sleep, Anesthesia prevents wake-up from damage
