====== Hero Subclass - Code References ======

===== Overview =====
Hero subclasses are mastery paths unlocked via Tome of Mastery after defeating the Tengu boss (boss #2). Each subclass provides unique abilities and class-specific armor.

===== Available Subclasses =====
  * Gladiator, Berserker (Warrior)
  * Warlock, BattleMage (Mage)
  * Assassin, FreeRunner (Rogue)
  * Sniper, Warden (Huntress)
  * Scout, Shaman (Elf)
  * Lich, WitchDoctor (Necromancer)
  * Guardian (Gnoll)
