    * `hasSlot(Slot slot)` - Checks if slot is available
    * `encumbranceCheck()` - Checks for STR requirement violations

    * `observe()` - Marks items as observed for badges
    * `uncurseEquipped()` / `curseEquipped()` - Curse management on equipped items
    * `dropAll()` - Drops all items
  * Equipment Slots (enum):
