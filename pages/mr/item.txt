===== Code Details =====
  * **Base class**: `com.watabou.pixeldungeon.items.Item`
  * **Key methods**: `collect()`, `detach()`, `price()`, `isKnown()`, `doPickUp()`, `doDrop()`, `execute()`
  * **Properties**: `image` (sprite index), `labelIndex` (potion/scroll identification), `defaultAction`, `cursed`, `cursedKnown`
  * **Identification system**: Items start unidentified; identified via Scroll of Identify or other means
  * **Cursed items**: Cannot be unequipped until uncursed
  * **Upgrade system**: Weapons and armor can be upgraded with Scroll of Upgrade (+1 to +10+, with risks)
  * **Glyphs/Enchantments**: Weapons and armor can be enchanted with glyphs for special effects
