===== Mechanics =====
**Parent Class:** Gnoll (HeroClass.GNOLL)

**Special Ability - Mana Shield on Spell Cast:**
  * Trigger: When the WitchDoctor casts any spell
  * Effect: Applies ManaShield buff to self
  * Duration: Equal to hero's skill level
  * Buff: "ManaShield" (custom buff)
  * Code: `Buff.affect(caster, "ManaShield", caster.skillLevel());` in HeroSubClass.spellCasted() switch case

**Class Armor:** WitchdoctorArmor (extends GnollArmor)
  * Visual: image=27, hasHelmet=true, coverHair=true, coverFacialHair=true
  * Equip restriction: Only Gnoll hero class can equip
  * Inherits DOMINATE special ability from GnollArmor

**Unlock Method:**
  * Via Tome of Mastery after defeating Boss #2 (Tengu)
  * Special item Tengu Liver can also provide subclass choice for Gnoll class
