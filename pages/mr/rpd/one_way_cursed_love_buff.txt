====== One Way Cursed Love Buff (Machine-Readable Reference) ======

==== Entity Identification ====
  * **Entity Kind**: OneWayCursedLoveBuff
  * **Entity Type**: Buff (Status Effect)
  * **Lua Script**: `scripts/buffs/OneWayCursedLoveBuff.lua`
  * **Package**: `scripts.buffs`
  * **Parent Artifact**: `DriedRose` (quest item, implemented in `scripts/items/DriedRose.lua`)
  * **Base Buff Class**: `ArtifactBuff` (via `attachArtifactBuff` mechanism)

==== Core Properties ====
  * **Buff Type**: Negative/Debuff (cursed variant)
  * **Icon**: `BuffIndicator.CURSED_ROSE` (icon 31)
  * **Source**: Cursed Dried Rose artifact
  * **Duration**: Permanent while cursed Dried Rose is equipped
  * **Removable**: Only by removing curse from Dried Rose or unequipping

==== Mechanics ====
  * **Effect**: One-way charm - causes NPCs to interact differently with player
  * **Charm Resistance**: In `CharUtils.charmDurationFactor` (Java) and `Charm.lua` (Lua):
    * If target has `OneWayLoveBuff`: charm power = 0 (immune to charm)
    * If target has `OneWayCursedLoveBuff`: charm power = 2 (enhanced charm effect)
  * **Quest Related**: Part of Ghost NPC questline
  * **Artifact Buff**: Attached via `RPD.ItemUtils:attachArtifactBuff` when cursed Dried Rose is equipped
