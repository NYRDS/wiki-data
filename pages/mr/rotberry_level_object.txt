===== Mechanics =====
**Activation Effect (WandMaker.Rotberry.effect):**
  * Creates toxic gas cloud at plant's location: `RPD.placeBlob(RPD.Blobs.ToxicGas, pos, 100)`
  * Applies Roots buff to activator: `Buff.prolong(target, "Roots", 3f)`
  * Drops a Rotberry Seed at the plant's position
  * Removes the Rotberry level object from the level

**Rotberry Seed (WandMaker.Rotberry.Seed):**
  * Item that can be planted to grow a new Rotberry level object
  * Image: ItemSpriteSheet.ROTBERRY_SEED (items/artifacts.png, index)
