===== Mechanics =====
**Encounter:**
  * Spawns in Prison level (depths 6-9) with 1 in (10 - depth) chance
  * Single encounter per game
  * Protected by magic shield (invulnerable to damage)

**Services:**
  * **Wand Upgrading:** Enhances wand quality in exchange for Rotberry Seed
  * **Free Identification:** Identifies all wands in inventory when upgrading
  * **Quest Rewards:** Offers choice between Battle Wands and Utility Wands

**Quest Types:**
  * Rotberry quest: Requires Rotberry Seed
  * Corpse Dust quest: Requires Corpse Dust

**Rewards:**
  * Battle Wands: Firebolt, Lightning, Poison, Frost, Magic Missile
  * Utility Wands: Amok, Regrowth, Slowness, Flock
  * All reward wands are randomly upgraded before giving
