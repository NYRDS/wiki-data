===== Implementation Details =====
  * **Class**: `com.watabou.pixeldungeon.items.potions.UpgradablePotion`
  * **Extends**: `Potion`
  * **Package**: `com.watabou.pixeldungeon.items.potions`
  * **Purpose**: Provides upgrade/degrade mechanics for potions
  * **Key Method**: `qualityFactor()` returns `Math.pow(2, level())` - volume scales exponentially with upgrade level
  * **Upgrade Behavior**: When upgraded, creates a new upgraded potion if quantity > 1
  * **Degrade Behavior**: When degraded, creates a new degraded potion if quantity > 1
  * **Random Generation**: 15% chance to be upgraded once, 2.25% chance to be upgraded twice
  * **Visual Indicator**: `visiblyUpgraded()` returns the potion's level
