===== Sources & Effects =====
**Sources that create Toxic Gas:**
  * **Potion of Toxic Gas** (`PotionOfToxicGas.java`) - Thrown potion creates toxic gas cloud (volume: 1000 * qualityFactor())
  * **Stench Glyph** (`Stench.java`) - Armor glyph triggers toxic gas on adjacent attacker (volume: 20, chance based on armor level)
  * **Rotten Food** (`RottenFood.java`) - Eating has 1/3 chance to create toxic gas (volume: 100)
  * **Toxic Trap** (`ToxicTrap.lua`) - Trap creates toxic gas (volume: 300 + 20 * depth)
  * **Corpse Explosion Spell** (`CorpseExplosion.lua`) - Consumes corpse to create toxic gas (volume based on corpse HP and caster skill)
  * **Remixed Fetid Rat** (`RemixedFetidRat.lua`) - Variant 3 releases toxic gas on death
  * **Lich** (`Lich.lua`) - Green variant releases toxic gas (volume: 30 * alive_skeletons)
  * **DM-300** (`DM300.lua`) - Boss constantly releases toxic gas (volume: 30)
  * **Rotberry Plant** (`Rotberry.lua`) - Activating releases toxic gas (volume: 100)
  * **Chaos Mark** (`ChaosCommon.java`) - Randomly spawns various gas blobs including ToxicGas

**Mechanics:**
  * Extends `Blob` class, implements `Doom` interface
  * In `evolve()`: deals poison damage to characters standing in gas
  * Damage formula: `(ch.ht() + levelDamage) / 40` where `levelDamage = 5 + Dungeon.depth * 5`, with 1/40 chance for +1 damage
  * Also poisons items on the ground (`heap.poison()`)
  * Interacts with ParalyticGas: when both gases overlap, the smaller volume is cancelled from both
  * Visual effect: `Speck.TOXIC` particles
  * Undead characters have immunity to ToxicGas (via `Char.setUndead()` adding `ToxicGas.class` to immunities)
  * GasesImmunity buff (`GasesImmunity.lua`) grants immunity to ToxicGas, Paralysis, and Vertigo
