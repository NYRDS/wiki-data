===== Sources & Effects =====
**Sources that create Miasma Gas:**
  * Various traps and enemy abilities

**Mechanics:**
  * Extends `Blob` class
  * In `evolve()`: applies three buffs to characters standing in gas:
    * **Poison** - Damage over time (duration: `CharUtils.durationFactor(ch) * 3f`)
    * **Weakness** - Reduced damage (duration: `CharUtils.durationFactor(ch) * 40f`)
    * **Vertigo** - Disorientation (duration: `CharUtils.durationFactor(ch) * 10f`)
  * Only affects characters without "ToxicGas" or "GasesImmunity" immunities
  * Visual effect: `Speck.MIASMA` particles
  * Interacts with ParalyticGas: when both gases overlap, the smaller volume is cancelled from both
