===== Weapon Stats =====
  * **Range**: 2 tiles (can attack enemies 2 tiles away)
  * **Block Slot**: LEFT_HAND (requires two hands)
  * **Animation**: SPEAR_ATTACK
  * **Flight**: Projectiles fly straight (isFliesStraight() returns true)
  * **Slot**: WEAPON (main hand)
