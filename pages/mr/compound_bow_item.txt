====== Compound Bow Item - Machine Readable Reference ======

===== Description =====
The **Compound Bow** is a ranged weapon that offers superior damage and accuracy compared to standard bows, though at the expense of attack speed.

===== Entity Information =====

  * **Type:** Weapon (Ranged)
