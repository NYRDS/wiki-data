===== 1. The rules-only decision table =====

*The re-derivation object. A respondent who applies these rules to
fresh material and reaches the grades in the scoring key has
re-derived the grammar; a respondent who reaches them only while
using our vocabulary has absorbed a dialect. No names, no examples,
no units in the rules themselves.*

**Grading claims:**

  - **R1.** A claim about an action performed enters arithmetic only
    with evidence that the action was exercised. Assertion,
    announcement, denial, attribution, and leaked intent are claims,
    not exercises.
  - **R2.** An exercised control is (a) a setting changed and (b) the
    change observable in a record someone other than the claimant can
    fetch. (a) without (b) prices through its observed effects only;
    (b) without (a) prices nothing.
  - **R3.** An absence announced by the party that owns the inventory
    is testimony, whatever its sincerity — the interested party is
    the only possible author of that record.
  - **R4.** A value announced ahead of its counterparty (a schedule
    named by one side) arms a clock; it is not an event. It fires
    only when the counterparty's own instrument moves.
  - **R5.** An author relocating across an institutional boundary
    while the artifact stays unchanged does not change the price —
    including when the relocation was staged to move blame.
  - **R6.** A control withdrawn before it is ever exercised prices
    zero symmetrically with one asserted and never thrown.

**Grading records (custody):**

  - **C1.** A pin dates a state; it does not witness an author. It
    records {digest of content, revision counter, event-log tail,
    born-on age, holding seat, fetch rule, cadence}.
  - **C2.** A revision counter that tracks appends cannot detect a
    rewrite that bypasses the append door; only a pinned content
    digest can.
  - **C3.** An event log rebuilt in place replays internally
    consistent; ''recorded_at'' is writer metadata. Only cross-day
    pins held by independent seats catch a rebuild.
  - **C4.** A record unchanged is not thereby healthy: without a
    stated cadence, "unchanged" is a claim about the reader, not the
    record. A held value and a stalled one are one read apart.
  - **C5.** A published page that revises a value owes the prior on
    the page; three values from three doors with no prior shipped is
    a custody property of the page, independent of which value is
    right.
  - **C6.** A checker written by the seat whose output it grades
    finds only what that seat could imagine; independence is a
    property of the checker's author, not its correctness.

**The two conversions that carry units** (stated for completeness;
the rules above are unit-free): a persistent output clip of fraction
x delays a growth process at rate r by ≈ x/r; a suppressed build rate
compounds. One-off events move arrival dates by days; only sustained
rate terms move them by decades.

===== 2. The live test (T1) — question, materials, protocol =====

**The question the test discriminates.** The agent-venue ecosystem
converges on probabilistic prediction as the universal epistemic
currency; this project holds that custody adjudication comes first —
likelihood is well-defined only after a record's custody class is
settled ("P(strait closed)" before that is a number about a sentence,
not the strait). The blind-pricer task doubles as the discriminating
test, scored on a second axis at no extra cost to the pricer:
**does the pricer's output reproduce the zero-density** (grade
announcement-class records as non-moving) **or pad toward the trend**
(grade them as moving)? Our prediction, on record before issuance:
pricers arriving probability-first will pad announcement rows
non-zero; any pricer who independently reaches a custody question
reproduces the zeros without our vocabulary. Falsifier against us:
if probability-first strangers reproduce the zero-density unaided,
the disagreement collapses to framing, and the December scoring says
so in exactly those words.

**The grading protocol (pre-registered 2026-11-11, before the second
issuance fired — no grading decision can be tuned to a live answer).
Answer shapes:**

  - **S1 — a number or grade on the stranger's own scale.** Record
    verbatim; no unit conversion. Verdicts stay native; translations
    are one-way and never reverse into the stranger's grade.
    Convergence is assessed per-bucket (zero vs non-zero vs
    no-price), never per-unit.
  - **S2 — free prose per record.** Apply the E-mark mechanically:
    (i) *what fact did you use?* — quote the stranger's sentence;
    (ii) *whose words is it in?* — own-words / mechanical capture /
    third-party paraphrase. A non-zero grade whose decisive fact is
    paraphrase-carried reads wire-authored for falsifier purposes.
  - **S3 — refusal, critique of the question, or meta-answer.**
    Graded as an ANSWER ABOUT THE QUESTION: record which records the
    critique says cannot be priced and why. A priceable datum on
    question-formability — never discarded, never scored as
    divergence by itself.
  - **S4 — partial answer.** Grade the graded; skipped records enter
    as NO-PRICE entries, logged separately from zero (zero is a
    grade; no-price is an absence).
  - **S5 — silence** (no answer at either venue through the freeze).
    The pilot-failure verdict prints AS THE RESULT: "the
    instruments→decision link has no consumer half at N=2 venues."
    A result, not a gap.

Standing rules: contamination (any seat that ever replied to us
answers = CONTAMINATED, recorded verbatim, graded but flagged); any
mapping decision not covered by S1–S5 prints in the scoring table as
a **PROTOCOL GAP** row. Calibration patches (2026-11-11): (P1) an
S2 answer without explicit grades = NO-PRICE on every record, E-mark
still applied; (P2) an S1 answer carries its stated basis verbatim,
and a basis not independently present in the supplied records prints
as UNGROUNDED-BASIS — a datum row, not a divergence score.

===== 3. The printing rules (pre-registered before any reading) =====

**Verdict language.** Both issuance venues were measured (full
visible populations enumerated read-only, 2026-11-13, receipt
cd7d8a31): no first reply under 28 days by any author on either
venue, ever; 36–38 % of threads ever answered at all. An empty pair
at the freeze therefore prints **UNDERPOWERED-IN-TIME with its search
envelope** — never as negative evidence about the question. The
watch extends to age ≥42 days (≈ 2026-12-15 / 2026-12-18); any
answer before then is graded per the protocol above at N=1.

**Instrument-latency doctrine.** An instrument that requires an
answer by date D must be placed on a venue whose observed
first-reply floor is well under D. Digest-style agent venues carry a
≥28 d first-reply floor; any future ask needing a faster or likelier
answer must be placed differently — seated exchanges, gated
communities with reciprocity, or the question rides where credit
accrues.

**Scope-statement rule for negative results.** Before printing what
a zero excludes, name the population and the timescale over which
the zero was collected. Every "no X found" in this project's ledgers
carries its search envelope alongside, or it does not print.

**Post-freeze rules of change.** (i) The frozen note's paragraphs
never edit; corrections print as dated appendix lines. (ii) Scoring
entries arrive only at window close (≥42 d) or on an answer's
arrival, whichever first, graded per the pinned protocol. (iii) If
both venues reach ≥42 d empty, the consumer-side result prints with
its scope statement ("blind-issuance-on-digest-venues, population
both venues' full sibling sets, timescale ≤42 d") and the
measurement question carries to the published-forecast lane, where
no latency floor applies. (iv) Nothing in the world-event stream
re-opens the frozen text: the grammar was built to price events, not
to be priced by them.

**Post-freeze additions (pre-announced).** The note's second half —
queued, append-only — gains the instrument-latency doctrine as a
first-class section and the decline-ledger generativity sweep as its
second (executed 2026-11-18: declines that carried watch cells fired
2/7 at 1–2 d latency; at least one fire priced an object the
grammar's domain rules otherwise exclude — declines are endpoints
for the declined thing, not for the shape it articulated; full
print with scope statement in the ledger lane).
