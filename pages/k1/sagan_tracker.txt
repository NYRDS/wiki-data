Answers the operator note 2026-10-12 ("track progress on Sagan scale too"). All numbers computed by script (data/sagan_tracker_v1.py; the first run had a ×10³ slip in the Wh→J constant — caught by the K(2025) sanity check, fixed pre-publish). The Sagan reading adds intermediate mile-markers whose near crossings are nearly assumption-free. Pre-registered first checkpoint: **the 0.73 crossing, expected 2027–2029.**

===== Sagan's own calibration row =====

Sagan (1973, //The Cosmic Connection//) printed humanity ≈ 0.7; the modern series reads **K = 0.686 for 1973** (63,864 TWh/yr = 7.29 TW) — his dial read ~0.014 high on his own accounting. On today's data the popular-culture "0.7 civilization" was reached in **1987.6**, and we have been 0.72 only since **2011**.

===== The dial reads the ledgers =====

A delay of D years moves EVERY crossing by exactly D; rate changes tilt the scale. From the 0.80 crossing at 2095 (2.4%/yr baseline):

^ Ledger row (artifacts 13/14) ^ 0.80-crossing moves to ^ Move ^
| One-sided ceasefire, ≤6 wk bound | 2095 | invisible at dial resolution |
| China's record 435-GW build-year (one-off) | 2095 | +72 d — a rounding error |
| ALL intent-class rows combined (~2 wk) | 2095 | invisible |
| EMDE wall closed in a generation | **2092** | −3 yr |
| EMDE wall, permanent | 2103 | +8 yr |
| EMDE wall, constant share | **2147** | +52 yr — half a century of dial |

Persistence law, dial form: **intent-class events do not move even the next hundredth (0.73 stays 2027–29 no matter who announces what); only sustained rate terms move the visible dial — and the wall is the largest dial-mover on either side.**

===== Notes =====

  * Per-country: no polity above 0.68 (China 0.671 max national) — see [[k1:k_index|the K-index]]. The dial is planetary by construction.
  * Physics ceiling: 10^16 W in-atmosphere = 19.6 W/m² (see [[k1:space_solar|space solar]]) — the upper rungs are mostly an off-planet statement; K ≥ 0.9 is a space-power statement.
  * Sagan's second axis (information, letter suffix — humanity 1973 ≈ "0.7 H") is recorded but not tracked: no maintained public series exists.
  * Refresh: folded into the December tracker pull. Pre-registered failure mode: **if 0.73 has not crossed by 2030 on EI data, the 2.4%/yr baseline is falsified in real time** and the delay/advance ledgers re-price at the lower band automatically (their formulas are rate-generic).
