## Two thresholds for engineering gain (the specie frame, Python-checked 2026-09-26)

Q_site = G × η_d × η_th (G = physics gain, η_d = driver wall-plug, η_th =
thermal-electric conversion; net electric = η_th·E_fusion − E_site).

- **Threshold 1 — net electricity, Q_site > 1.** Required G across the full
  declared driver range η_d = 0.20 → 0.10 at η_th=0.40: **25 … 16.7 … 13.9 …
  12.5** — i.e. **G > 12.5–25 = ×3.0–6.1 above demonstrated G=4.13** [v1.2
  fix (d); v1.1 printed only 12.5–16.7]. Same fold at the wall: net
  electricity needs raw ratio 1/η_th = **2.5–2.9 → ×86–130 from demonstrated
  laser-wall 0.022–0.029** [v1.2 fix (e)].
- **Threshold 2 — economic, Q_site ≳ 5** (recirculating fraction 1/Q ≤ 20%;
  fossil/nuclear house loads run 5–10%). Required G: **62.5–125** = **×15–30
  above demonstrated** [span corrected v1.2; v1.1 printed 62.5–83.3].
- **Where we actually stand:** demonstrated G=4.13 at demonstrated η_d≈0.0059
  [2.08 MJ light ÷ 350 MJ wall] and η_th=0.40 → **Q_site ≈ 0.010**. The driver
  lever alone (0.0059 → 0.15–0.20) is worth ×25–34 on Q_site: same target
  gain at design drivers = Q_site 0.25–0.33. Gain AND driver efficiency must
  each climb roughly an order of magnitude.
- **The rate mountain (unchanged, now with denominators):** 10 Hz = 8.64×10^5
  shots/day [Betti: ~9×10^5 targets/day] vs NIF ~2/day [~700/yr] = **×4.3×10^5
  on cadence** (v1 said "~10^6×" — corrected down with the denominator
  printed; PESS direction).
