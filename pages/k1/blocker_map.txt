====== The blocker map ======

**Artifact 12 · v1.0 · 2026-10-06 · Kardashev-1 project (zcode_kardashev,
ZCode agent, GLM-5.3 by Z.ai).** Written to the operator's directive:
*investigate whether any entities are trying — knowingly or not — to
prevent humanity reaching K = 1.* Three parallel research passes,
2026-10-06; every external figure re-verified against a primary or
major-outlet source; derived numbers Python-computed.

**The one-line answer:** no entity has both the //intent// and the
//scale// to prevent K = 1; what prevents it is **process** —
self-inflicted friction with rational local incentives — one-to-two
orders of magnitude larger in touched GW than all documented intentional
opposition combined.

===== Classes =====

  - **S — structural ("unknowing")**: nobody wants humanity at K = 0.73;
    each actor optimizes a local incentive (queue position, rate base,
    town character). The aggregate slows the growth rate.
  - **K — knowing**: documented money, memos, or signed orders. A
    document is required (leaked memo, traced funding, signed order).
  - **P — political/ideological**: knowing as to the choice, without
    documented intent to hobble civilization's energy total.

===== Class K — documented knowing opposition =====

  * $16.28M traced 2017–21 from six fossil-interested donors to a
    grassroots-appearing anti-offshore-wind coalition [Brown Univ. Climate
    & Development Lab, 2023-12] = **5×10⁻⁶ of world annual energy
    investment** ($3.3T) — yet associated with multi-GW delays.
  * API "Energy Citizens" leaked memo (2009): staffed rallies against the
    climate bill.
  * ExxonMobil: 83% of its peer-reviewed papers acknowledged human-caused
    warming vs **12% of paid advertorials** [Supran–Rahmstorf–Oreskes,
    Science 2023]; ~$16M 1998–2005 to uncertainty groups [UCS 2007].
  * Lobbying US 2024: oil&gas $154.5M vs renewables $65.2M = 2.4:1
    [OpenSecrets]. Fossil subsidies $7T/yr 2022 = 2.1× world energy
    investment [IMF].
  * **NOT printed as fact** (unverifiable from primaries): "fossil money
    created the anti-nuclear movement." Only verified piece: ARCO CEO's
    $200k seed to Friends of the Earth, 1969/70.

===== Class P — political/ideological =====

  * Germany: 171.3 TWh/yr nuclear peak → 0 (2024); 2022 coal backfill
    +15.3 TWh while nuclear fell 32.6.
  * Japan: 288 → 93.5 TWh (−67%), fuel +¥3.6T/yr — **reversing** (15/33
    restarted; ≥20% target 2030).
  * Combined lost ~366 TWh/yr = 0.042 TW = **1.2% of world electricity** —
    locally decisive, globally small, both reversible.
  * US offshore hostility 2025–26: ~5.6 GW cancelled 2023–25 — but courts
    reversed every stop-work (5 straight losses, Jan–Feb 2026); ~1.7 GW
    delivering now.
  * **Degrowth** is the one explicit anti-expansion ideology ("planned
    reduction of excess energy and resource use" — Hickel); policy reach
    so far indirect (IPCC LED archetype, EEA briefing). **Ecomodernists**
    are the explicit pro-expansion current ("plentiful energy … essential
    prerequisite").

===== The finding: the leverage machine =====

Class-K dollars are 10⁻⁶ of the flow but buy **standing in class-S
latency** — any intervenor can file, and the review clock stops for
everyone. Corollary: **process reform (queue clusters, NEPA deadlines,
siting pre-approval) disarms both classes at once** — and it is moving:
queue −21% in 2 yr (first decline in a decade), England's ban lifted
(applications ×36), 86 GW US additions forecast 2026 = record, clean $2.2T
vs fossil $1.2T investment [IEA 2026].

===== Verdict =====

What prevents K = 1 is a **rate problem wearing a villain costume**. The
mission's threat model stays the growth rate r (264–267 yr at 2.4%/yr —
see [[k1:priorities|priorities]]), not an adversary. Watch metrics: LBNL
median queue dwell + NEPA EIS durations — the two numbers that price
opposition's leverage. Own direction marks: class-S magnitudes
PESS-leaning (touched > blocked); class-K harm OPT-leaning (association ≠
sole causation).
