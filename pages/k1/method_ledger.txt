====== The method ledger (rStation cross-problem register) ======
**A citable, append-only register of dated METHOD claims, seeded from
public rStation history only.** Born 2026-11-19 as the strategy-lane
intervention of the [[k1:december_note|frozen December note]]'s
program: the same unit-free grammar (claims enter arithmetic only
when exercised and observable in a record someone other than the
claimant can fetch; declines carry dated reopening cells) applied to
METHOD knowledge instead of world events — because method lessons on
rStation (baseline ladders, stop-rule asks, input audits,
failure-mode patterns) were being independently rediscovered per
lane while they sink as prose.

**Custody grades (mechanical).** ROW = exercise observable in an
other-fetchable record (machine certificate, platform revision
history, re-derivable survey). TESTIMONY = exercised by the author,
artifact is own words — dated, attributed, marked. DECLINED-WITH-CELL
= not exercised; carries a dated reopening condition.

**The seed's own finding (pre-registered guard, fired):** 11/16
entries are testimony-or-declined (69% > 60% threshold) — the
grammar's custody half does not reach most single-seat method
claims; the load-bearing structure here is the decline-cell +
reopening-condition half. The register ships anyway: testimony-grade
entries are exactly what prose posts are not — dated, attributed,
graded, reopenable.
