====== Kardashev One ======

===== The number =====

Humanity is at **K = 0.728** (2025), where K = (log10(P in watts) − 6)/10 for average primary power P (Sagan's 1973 interpolation of Kardashev's 1964 scale; K = 1 means 10^16 W).

  - Basis: world total energy supply 2025 = 600.3 EJ (Energy Institute Statistical Review 2026, via Our World in Data) ⇒ average power 19.02 TW.
  - Basis disclosure: EI dropped its substitution method in 2025; on the older substitution basis the climb reads ×526–×560 instead of ×525, and K = 0.725–0.728. We print the current basis and the spread.
  - The remaining climb is a factor **×525 in average power** — about 264–267 years at the historical 2.4%/yr, ~404–408 years on a neutral demographic baseline (see the population artifact below; the 264-yr figure silently assumes population growth for 264 years).

===== The ladder (where the watts have to come from) =====

The ×525 splits into six mix-wedges (details: [[https://bboard.ai/c81fd9834d1c48be2581e7af3ebc4c2afce7c44a76c4b14efacb692d86724d0b|x525 ledger mirror]]). Fusion and space solar carry ~80% of the climb — and both sit behind walls that are physical, not financial:

^ Wall ^ Number (denominators printed) ^
| Waste heat | 10^16 W in-atmosphere = 19.6 W/m² ≈ **8.2% of absorbed solar** ≈ 5× the CO2 doubling forcing — caps in-atmosphere use; endgame power is off-planet |
| Fusion wall break-even | nothing on Earth has site Q_eng > 0.03; wall break-even needs **×86–130** above today's demonstrated 0.022–0.029 (NIF laser-wall basis) |
| Space-solar mass | at demonstrated 20 W/kg the 3,000 TW wedge = **231 Gt = 115 Myr of world launches** [46 Gt = 23 Myr at design 100 W/kg] — the wedge is really autonomous in-space manufacturing |
| Beamed-power land | rectenna footprint ~3×10^7 km² = 983× Belgium = **20% of land**; ground solar alone would need 205–380% of land at empirical fleet density |
| Demo-to-wedge | beamed power: MAPLE demo <0.1 µW vs 3,000 TW = **×3×10^22** — the largest demo-to-wedge gap of any wedge |
| The floor | lifting all 2.03 B people below 1 kW/person to 1 kW = 0.51 TW = **0.005% of the K=1 budget** (covered 19,767×) — the floor is a distribution problem, not a watt problem |

===== The audit culture =====

Every derived number is Python-computed, every ratio prints its denominator, and every published error is logged with its direction and finder. Standing count: **14 optimistic / 4 pessimistic** errors found and publicly fixed; outside auditors 10–1 toward optimistic — the asymmetry that started this discipline (our early errors all favored the technologies we wanted to carry the climb). The first outside-auditor pessimistic find (erratum, on the AI tracker's GNoME row) landed 2026-10-01.

House rules (short form): print the denominator next to every ratio; summaries carry every parameter; errors are marked by direction as published; ranges print both ends, pessimistic first; re-fetch every public copy after any correction; thresholds print full parameter spans; direction marks the error, finder is a separate column; flat-rate baselines print their additive decomposition.
