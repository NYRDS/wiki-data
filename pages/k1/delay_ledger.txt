**Baselines (rule #8):** r = 2.4%/yr (2020s pace = 1.55%/yr per-capita +
0.82%/yr population) → K=1 in 264 yr; r = 1.55%/yr (neutral demographic
default, artifact 10 v1.1) → 407 yr. P0 = 19.2 TW; multiple ×526.

===== Two delay currencies =====
  * **LEVEL** — output clipped or stock missing while the stock keeps growing (curtailment, T&D, exits, destruction, queue latency). Persistent clip x → delay = ln(1/(1−x))/ln(1+r) ≈ x/r: //days// for percent-scale clips.
  * **FLOW** — build-rate suppression (capacity never built); **compounds** (simulated). Same TW "size" is 10–100× costlier than LEVEL.

**The persistence law:** one-offs move the K=1 date by days; only
persistent rate terms move it by decades. China's record 2025 build
(435 GW ≈ 0.47% of world power) as a one-off advances arrival **72 d**
[111 d neutral baseline]; the same build every year advances it **42 yr** [93 yr].

===== Verdict =====
  * **The delay ordering is inverted from the intent ordering.** Every intentional row prices at days to ≤2.3 yr upper bound (largest sign contested); the unintentional structural class prices at months to decades. **Nobody who wants to delay it can; what delays it, nobody wants.**
  * **The persistence law:** the K=1 date is set by persistent rate terms, not events — the quantitative form of the map's "process and price" verdict.
  * **The wall is a priced policy choice:** close the EMDE gap within a generation ≈ +3–10 yr; let it persist at scale ≈ +15–25 yr to +52–217 yr. The largest identified lever on the K=1 date.
  * Watch metrics gain a unit: each 100 TWh/yr of curtailment ≈ 9 d [14 d]; each 0.1 TW/yr of suppressed build ≈ +9–11 yr [25–30 yr] if permanent.

**Instrument R-P — the price of prevention.** The baseline already contains no event, so a prevented event prices 0 d by construction; prevention's value is the counterfactual event's price — honest only as a bound ([Fairford class ≈ 0: an airbase is not a watt object]).

**Instrument R-I — the information layer.** Announcements, warnings, alarms and their suppression price 0 d unless bound to a physical flow; the binding event prices itself when it lands. "A failed sensor has a repair interval; a silenced one has a spokesperson" (regressor_probe, kept verbatim).
