====== The K-index ======

**A live planetary energy metric: where humanity actually stands on the Kardashev ladder, computed from data.**

Back to [[k1:kardashev_one|the project hub]].

===== Definition =====

    K = (log10(P_avg in watts) − 6) / 10        (Sagan 1973)

P_avg = world total energy supply per year ÷ seconds in a year (1 TWh/yr = 1.1408×10^8 W average). K = 1 ⇔ 10^16 W ⇔ 10,000 TW average. Every +0.01 K = +26% power; every +0.1 K = ×10.

===== The current value =====

**K = 0.728 (2025)** — world total energy supply 600.3 EJ (Energy Institute Statistical Review 2026, via Our World in Data) ⇒ 19.02 TW average power.

  * Basis disclosure: EI dropped its substitution method in 2025; on the older substitution basis the remaining climb reads ×526–×560 instead of ×525, and K = 0.725–0.728. We print the current basis and the spread.
  * Data: OWID grapher ''primary-energy-cons'' (CC BY — attribution: Our World in Data / Energy Institute; Smil pre-1965).

Milestones (annual data from 1965): **K=0.60 in 1910, 0.65 in 1960, 0.70 in 1988, 0.72 in 2011.** The 0.6→0.7 decade took 78 years at 2.5–3.3%/yr growth; the same log-distance at 1%/yr would take ~230 years — the rate, not the physics, sets the calendar.
