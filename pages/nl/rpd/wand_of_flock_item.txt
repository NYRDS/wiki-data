====== Wand of Flock ======

{{ rpd:images:wand_of_flock_item.png|Wand of Flock }}

The **Wand of Flock** is a utility wand that summons a flock of magic sheep to create temporary obstacles.

==== Item Overview ====
  * **Type:** [[en:rpd:wand_item|Wand]]
  * **Rarity:** Uncommon
  * **Special Effect:** Summons magic sheep that block movement

==== Special Mechanics ====
  * Summons (wand level + 2) sheep
  * Sheep disappear after (wand level + 3) turns

==== Strategy ====
  * Excellent for blocking passages during combat

==== Obtaining ====
  * Found in shops and dropped by enemies
