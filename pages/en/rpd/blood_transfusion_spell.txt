====== Blood Transfusion ======

==== Description ====
**Blood Transfusion** is a Plague Doctor affinity spell that allows the caster to drain health from a friendly character (including summoned allies) to heal themselves. The spell transfers a portion of the target's health to the caster, with the transfer efficiency scaling with the caster's skill level.

==== Mechanics ====
  * **Magic Affinity**: PlagueDoctor
  * **Targeting Type**: Character (not self) — must target an ally or summoned minion
  * **Spell Level**: 2
  * **Cast Time**: 1 turn
  * **Mana Cost**: 2
  * **Cooldown**: 1 turn

==== Effects ====
When cast on a valid target (not the caster themselves):
  * **Drain**: Removes HP from the target equal to the amount needed to fully heal the caster, capped at the target's current HP
  * **Transfer Rate**: Scales with the caster's skill level:
    * Level 1: 75% transfer efficiency
    * Level 5: 95% transfer efficiency
    * Level 6+: 100% transfer efficiency
  * **Heal**: The caster receives the transferred HP (drain × transfer rate)
  * **Visuals**: Blood particles on the target, Elmo particles on the caster
  * **Sound**: Healing sound effect
  * **Messages**: "BloodTransfusion_Drained" on success, "BloodTransfusion_WontAgreed" if target is invalid, "BloodTransfusion_FullHP" if caster is already at full health

==== Restrictions ====
  * Cannot be cast on self
  * Cannot be cast on hostile enemies
  * Fails if caster is already at full HP
  * Target must be a character owned by the caster (ally, summon, pet)

==== Strategy ====
  * Best used with durable summons (e.g., Skeleton, Deathling) that can survive the HP drain
  * The transfer rate scaling makes this spell significantly more effective at higher skill levels
  * Useful emergency heal when no potions are available and you have expendable allies
  * The short cooldown (1 turn) allows repeated use if you have multiple allies or a high-HP summon
