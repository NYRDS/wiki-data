====== Liquid Flame ======

{{ rpd:images:potion_of_liquid_flame_item.png|Liquid Flame effect }}

**Liquid Flame** is a harmful environmental effect in Remixed Pixel Dungeon that deals damage to characters standing on it.

==== Description ====
Liquid Flame is a blob effect that creates a fire hazard on the dungeon floor, dealing continuous fire damage to any character on the affected tile. It spreads to adjacent flammable terrain and can be created by various sources, including the Potion of Liquid Flame.

==== Mechanics ====
  * **Damage:** Deals fire damage each turn to all creatures in the affected cell
  * **Spread:** Can spread to adjacent flammable terrain tiles
  * **Duration:** Persists for several turns before fading
  * **Resistance:** Characters with fire resistance take reduced damage
