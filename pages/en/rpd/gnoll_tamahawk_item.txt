====== Gnoll Tomahawk ======

{{ rpd:images:gnoll_tamahawk_item.png|Gnoll Tomahawk }}

==== Description ====
The **Gnoll Tomahawk** is a traditional throwing weapon crafted by Gnoll masters, where each clan has its own unique design. This tomahawk is slightly lighter than its human-made counterparts, making it more suitable for throwing.

==== Stats ====
  * **Type:** Missile Weapon (Throwable)
  * **Damage:** 3-17 (average 10)
  * **Strength Requirement:** 15 STR
  * **Range:** Standard missile weapon range
  * **Special Effect:** Causes **Bleeding** on hit (level equal to damage dealt)
  * **Stackable:** Yes (quantity 5-12 when randomly generated)
  * **Upgradable:** Yes
  * **Identified:** Yes (always identified)
  * **Price:** 20 gold per unit

==== Special Mechanics ====
  * **Bleeding Effect:** On successful hit, applies Bleeding buff to the target with level equal to the damage dealt
  * **Throwing Weapon:** Default action is Throw; can be equipped by Huntress, Rogue, and Gnoll classes without confirmation
  * **Other Classes:** Other hero classes can equip it but will receive a confirmation prompt
  * **Random Variants:** Each Gnoll Tomahawk has a random visual variant (9 different appearances from gnoll_tomahawks.png sprite sheet)
  * **Stacking:** Multiple tomahawks stack in a single inventory slot (stored in Quiver)

==== Acquisition ====
  * Starting weapon for the **Gnoll** hero class
  * Found in Treasury and Spiders Treasury levels (spawn weight 0 - very rare)
  * Can be obtained as random loot

==== Strategy ====
  * **Primary Ranged Weapon:** Excellent for Gnoll, Huntress, and Rogue classes who can equip it freely
  * **Bleeding Synergy:** The bleeding effect deals additional damage over time, effective against high-HP enemies
  * **Lightweight:** Lower strength requirement (15) than standard Tomahawk (17), making it accessible earlier
  * **Stack Management:** Since they stack, carry multiple for extended ranged combat
  * **Upgradable:** Can be upgraded with Scrolls of Upgrade to increase damage

==== Class Restrictions ====
  * **Can Equip Freely:** [[en:rpd:gnoll_class|Gnoll]], [[en:rpd:huntress_class|Huntress]], [[en:rpd:rogue_class|Rogue]]
  * **Confirmation Required:** All other classes (Warrior, Mage, Elf, Necromancer, Priest, Doctor)
