====== Glyph of Bounce ======

The Glyph of Bounce is an [[en:rpd:armor_glyphs|armor glyph]] that can appear on certain types of armor in Remixed Dungeon. This glyph provides knockback-based defensive abilities to the wearer.

==== Description ====
When an enemy hits the wearer of armor with this glyph, there is a chance that the attacker will be knocked back, creating distance between the hero and the enemy.

==== Mechanics ====
Grounded in [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/scripts/glyphs/Bounce.lua|Bounce.lua]]:
  * **Activation:** Triggers when the wearer is hit by an adjacent enemy
  * **Probability:** Chance based on armor level, (armor level + 5) >= 4, scaling with armor level
  * **Effect:** The attacker is pushed away from the defender
  * **Limitations:** Only works against movable enemies that are adjacent to the wearer

==== Armor Compatibility ====
  * Can appear on any armor as a random glyph inscription

==== Strategy ====
  * Effective for creating space when surrounded by enemies
  * Can push enemies into traps or chasms
  * Particularly useful against melee enemies
  * Works well with ranged combat strategies

==== Identification ====
  * Armor with this glyph appears as normal until activated
  * Can be identified through various means like [[en:rpd:scroll_of_identify_item|Scroll of Identify]]

==== Acquisition ====
  * [[en:rpd:armor_item|Armor]] randomly generated with this glyph
  * [[en:rpd:scroll_of_enchantment_item|Scroll of Enchantment]]
  * [[en:rpd:artificer_npc|Artificer NPC]] services
