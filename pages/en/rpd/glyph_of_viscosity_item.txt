====== Glyph of Viscosity ======

The Glyph of Viscosity is an [[en:rpd:armor_glyphs|armor glyph]] that can appear on certain types of armor in Remixed Dungeon. This glyph defers incoming damage into a slow drain over time instead of taking it all at once.

==== Description ====
When an enemy hits the wearer of armor with this glyph, there is a chance that the damage will be deferred, converting the immediate damage into a damage-over-time effect that ticks for 1 damage per turn.

==== Mechanics ====
Grounded in [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/scripts/glyphs/Viscosity.lua|Viscosity.lua]]:
  * **Activation:** Triggers on non-zero damage, chance (armor level + 7) >= 6
  * **Effect:** Negates the incoming damage entirely and applies DeferredDamage buff
  * **Deferred Damage:** The buff accumulates the prevented damage and deals 1 damage per turn
  * **Stacking:** Multiple activations add to the existing DeferredDamage buff level
  * **First Tick Delay:** The first damage tick is delayed by one game tick
  * **Visual:** Shows "deferred X" warning message with the amount

==== Armor Compatibility ====
  * Can appear on any armor as a random glyph inscription

==== Strategy ====
  * Converts burst damage into manageable sustained damage
  * Allows survival against otherwise lethal single hits
  * Requires healing over time to outpace the damage drain
  * Dangerous if multiple large hits are deferred simultaneously
  * Works well with regeneration effects

==== Identification ====
  * Armor with this glyph appears as normal until activated
  * Can be identified through various means like [[en:rpd:scroll_of_identify_item|Scroll of Identify]]

==== Acquisition ====
  * [[en:rpd:armor_item|Armor]] randomly generated with this glyph
  * [[en:rpd:scroll_of_enchantment_item|Scroll of Enchantment]]
  * [[en:rpd:artificer_npc|Artificer NPC]] services
