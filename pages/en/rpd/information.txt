====== Information ======

In Remixed Dungeon, **Information** refers to the knowledge and hints that players can gather from various sources throughout the dungeon. NPCs, books, scrolls, and environmental clues all provide valuable information that can help players survive and progress.

==== Sources of Information ====

==== NPCs ====
Several NPCs provide information through dialogue:
  * **[[en:rpd:ghost_npc|Ghost]]** - Offers quests and hints about the dungeon's history
  * **[[en:rpd:imp_npc|Imp]]** - Provides quests with rewards
  * **[[en:rpd:wandmaker_npc|Wandmaker]]** - Shares knowledge about wands and magic
  * **[[en:rpd:shopkeeper_npc|Shopkeeper]]** - May offer rumors or tips

==== Written Sources ====
  * **Books and Tomes** - Found in libraries and special rooms, contain lore and mechanical hints
  * **Scrolls** - Some scrolls provide information when read (e.g., Scroll of Magic Mapping)
  * **Signs and Inscriptions** - Environmental text that provides clues

==== Game Mechanics ====
  * **[[en:rpd:identification|Identification]]** - Reveals properties of unknown items
  * **[[en:rpd:magic_mapping|Magic Mapping]]** - Reveals level layout
  * **[[en:rpd:mind_vision|Mind Vision]]** - Reveals enemies and items on the level

==== Strategic Value ====
Information gathering is crucial for:
  * Understanding enemy weaknesses and behaviors
  * Locating hidden rooms and secrets
  * Learning about item properties before using them
  * Progressing through quests
  * Making informed tactical decisions
