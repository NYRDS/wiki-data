====== Gladiator Subclass ======

{{ rpd:images:warrior_gladiator_hero.png|Gladiator }}

**Gladiator** is a hero subclass for the [[en:rpd:warrior_class|Warrior]] class in Remixed Dungeon. The Gladiator specializes in combo-based melee combat, building up damage with each successful hit.

==== Description ====
The Gladiator subclass enhances the [[en:rpd:warrior_class|Warrior]]'s natural combat abilities with a combo mechanic. Each successful melee attack starts or continues a combo, in which every next successful hit inflicts more damage. This makes the Gladiator excel at sustained combat against tough enemies.

==== Abilities ====
  * **Combo Attacks:** A successful melee weapon attack allows the Gladiator to start a combo, in which every subsequent successful hit deals increasing damage
  * **Class Armor:** [[en:rpd:gladiator_armor_item|Gladiator Armor]] grants the "Heroic Leap" ability — jump to a target cell and stun adjacent enemies on landing
  * **Playstyle:** Aggressive melee combat with damage that scales during sustained fights

==== Unlocking ====
  1. Defeat [[en:rpd:tengu_mob|Tengu]] (boss of the Prison area)
  2. Obtain a [[en:rpd:tome_of_mastery_item|Tome of Mastery]]
  3. Read the Tome and choose "Gladiator"
