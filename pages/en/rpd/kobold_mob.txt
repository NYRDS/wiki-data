====== Kobold Mob ======

{{ rpd:images:kobold_mob.png|Kobold }}

**Kobold** is a common monster inhabiting the Ice Caves in Remixed Dungeon. Kobolds are considered to be mere legends. Being ancestors of regular Gnolls, Kobolds dwelled in the deepest and coldest caves. It is also said that their race mastered engineering and mechanisms long before Dwarves.

==== Mechanics ====
  * **Terror Immunity**: Kobolds are immune to the [[en:rpd:terror_buff|Terror]] effect
  * **Standard Enemy**: Has no special abilities or attacks
  * **Drop**: [[en:rpd:potion_of_frost_item|Potion of Frost]] can drop with a 10% chance

==== Stats ====
  * **Health (HP):** 60
  * **Attack Skill:** 21
  * **Defense Skill:** 18
  * **Damage:** 10-17
  * **DR:** 9
  * **Experience (EXP):** 10
  * **Max Level:** 20

==== Strategy ====
  * Kobolds are standard enemies without special abilities
  * Any attack type is effective against them
  * They don't pose a serious threat to a prepared hero
  * Can be dangerous in large groups

==== Habitat ====
  * [[en:rpd:ice_caves_level|Ice Caves]] — Primary spawn area
