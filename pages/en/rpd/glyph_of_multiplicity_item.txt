====== Glyph of Multiplicity ======

The Glyph of Multiplicity is an [[en:rpd:armor_glyphs|armor glyph]] that can appear on certain types of armor in Remixed Dungeon. This glyph creates a mirror image of the wearer when hit, at the cost of taking damage.

==== Description ====
When an enemy hits the wearer of armor with this glyph, there is a chance that a clone of the wearer will be created adjacent to them. The clone fights alongside the hero but the creation process damages the hero.

==== Mechanics ====
Grounded in [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/scripts/glyphs/Multiplicity.lua|Multiplicity.lua]]:
  * **Activation:** Chance based on armor level, (floor(armor level / 2) + 6) >= 5
  * **Effect:** Creates a clone of the wearer in an adjacent empty cell
  * **Cost:** The wearer takes 1 to floor(max HP / 6) damage from the cloning process
  * **For Heroes:** Creates a mirror image using the hero's clone ability
  * **For Other Entities:** Uses the standard split mechanic

==== Armor Compatibility ====
  * Can appear on any armor as a random glyph inscription

==== Strategy ====
  * The clone can distract enemies and deal additional damage
  * Self-damage can be dangerous at low HP - avoid when critically wounded
  * Most effective in open areas where there's space for the clone to spawn
  * Clones inherit the wearer's equipment and stats

==== Identification ====
  * Armor with this glyph appears as normal until activated
  * Can be identified through various means like [[en:rpd:scroll_of_identify_item|Scroll of Identify]]

==== Acquisition ====
  * [[en:rpd:armor_item|Armor]] randomly generated with this glyph
  * [[en:rpd:scroll_of_enchantment_item|Scroll of Enchantment]]
  * [[en:rpd:artificer_npc|Artificer NPC]] services
