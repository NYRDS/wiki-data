====== Glyph of Metabolism ======

The Glyph of Metabolism is an [[en:rpd:armor_glyphs|armor glyph]] that can appear on certain types of armor in Remixed Dungeon. This glyph converts hunger into healing when the wearer is hit.

==== Description ====
When an enemy hits the wearer of armor with this glyph, there is a chance that the wearer will heal by consuming their hunger (satiety). This trades long-term sustenance for immediate survival.

==== Mechanics ====
Grounded in [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/scripts/glyphs/Metabolism.lua|Metabolism.lua]]:
  * **Activation:** Chance based on armor level, (floor(armor level / 2) + 5) >= 4
  * **Healing:** Heals up to 1/5 of max HP, but not exceeding missing HP
  * **Cost:** Reduces hunger by 40 (equivalent to 10% of starving threshold)
  * **Limitation:** Does not activate if the wearer is already starving
  * **Visual:** Shows green healing numbers on activation

==== Armor Compatibility ====
  * Can appear on any armor as a random glyph inscription

==== Strategy ====
  * Excellent for emergency healing when potions are unavailable
  * Requires careful food management - don't rely on it exclusively
  * Most effective at high armor levels where activation chance is higher
  * Risky when food is scarce or in long dungeon runs

==== Identification ====
  * Armor with this glyph appears as normal until activated
  * Can be identified through various means like [[en:rpd:scroll_of_identify_item|Scroll of Identify]]

==== Acquisition ====
  * [[en:rpd:armor_item|Armor]] randomly generated with this glyph
  * [[en:rpd:scroll_of_enchantment_item|Scroll of Enchantment]]
  * [[en:rpd:artificer_npc|Artificer NPC]] services
