====== Secrets ======

Secrets in Remixed Dungeon include hidden doors, secret passages, hidden traps, and concealed level objects that are not visible until discovered by searching or by special abilities.

==== Finding Secrets ====
  * **Search:** Use the search action (toolbar button or `S` key) to reveal nearby secrets
  * **Ring of Detection:** Automatically reveals secrets without searching
  * **Magic Mapping:** Reveals layout and hidden doors on scroll reading
  * **Mind Vision:** Reveals hidden rooms and secret areas

==== Types ====
  * Hidden doors (secret passages between rooms)
  * Hidden traps (not visible until stepped on or detected)
  * Secret level objects (e.g. hidden traps on objects)
