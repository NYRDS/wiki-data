====== Plague Doctor Quest ======

The Plague Doctor Quest is a series of tasks from the Plague Doctor NPC in Remixed Dungeon. The doctor is found in the dungeon and asks players to bring various items in exchange for rewards.

==== Description ====
The Plague Doctor is an NPC that gives players quests to collect items. Each quest consists of a prologue, requirements, an in-progress stage, and a reward.

==== Quest List ====

==== Stage 1 ====
**Variant 1:**
  * **Task:** Bring 5 Rat Carcasses (Carcass of Rat)
  * **Reward:** Rat Armor (RatArmor)

**Variant 2:**
  * **Task:** Bring 10 Snail Carcasses (Carcass of Snail)
  * **Reward:** 50 Gold

==== Stage 2 ====
**Variant 1:**
  * **Task:** Bring 2 Moongrace Seeds (Moongrace.Seed) — [[en:rpd:moongrace_seed_item|Moongrace Seeds]]
  * **Reward:** [[en:rpd:mana_potion_item|Potion of Mana]]

**Variant 2:**
  * **Task:** Bring 2 Sungrass Seeds (Sungrass.Seed) — [[en:rpd:sungrass_seed_item|Sungrass Seeds]]
  * **Reward:** [[en:rpd:potion_of_healing_item|Potion of Healing]]

==== Stage 3 ====
**Variant 1:**
  * **Task:** Kill 1 Bat
  * **Reward:** [[en:rpd:scroll_of_upgrade_item|Scroll of Upgrade]]

**Variant 2:**
  * **Task:** Kill 1 Death Knight
  * **Reward:** [[en:rpd:potion_of_might_item|Potion of Might]]

==== Stage 4 ====
**Variant 1:**
  * **Task:** Bring 5 Warlock Carcasses (Carcass of Warlock)
  * **Reward:** [[en:rpd:potion_of_might_item|Potion of Might]]

**Variant 2:**
  * **Task:** Bring 5 Kobold Icemancer Carcasses (Carcass of KoboldIcemancer)
  * **Reward:** [[en:rpd:potion_of_might_item|Potion of Might]]

==== Stage 5 ====
**Variant 1:**
  * **Task:** Kill 2 Succubi
  * **Reward:** Special Reward

==== Mechanics ====
  * Quests are given one at a time
  * After completing a quest, the next one is given randomly
  * If the player is playing as the Doctor class, the NPC disappears
  * Quest progress is saved between sessions

==== Strategy ====
  * Moongrace Seeds are rare in deep caves — save them for the quest
  * Sungrass Seeds are more common — easier to find
  * Monster carcasses are collected automatically when killed
  * The "Potion of Mana" reward is valuable for classes that use spells
