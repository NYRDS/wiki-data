====== Alchemy ======

{{ rpd:images:firebloom_seed_item.png|Alchemy Seeds }}

Alchemy in Remixed Dungeon is a system for brewing potions by throwing seeds into the alchemy pot found in the [[en:rpd:sewers_level|Sewers]]. Unlike fixed recipes, the system randomly selects a potion based on the seeds thrown in.

==== Alchemy Mechanics ====
  * Alchemy is performed at an alchemy pot (cauldron) found in the [[en:rpd:sewers_level|Sewers Level]]
  * Throw 3 or more seeds into the pot to trigger transmutation
  * The pot randomly picks one seed (weighted by quantity) and brews its associated potion
  * If the chosen seed has no alchemy class, a random potion is created from the treasury
  * Each seed type has an **alchemyClass** property defining its potion (e.g., Sungrass → Potion of Healing)
  * Seeds without an alchemy class (e.g., Rotberry) yield a random potion

==== Seed-to-Potion Mapping ====
  * **Sungrass Seed** → [[en:rpd:potion_of_healing_item|Potion of Healing]]
  * **Firebloom Seed** → [[en:rpd:potion_of_liquid_flame_item|Potion of Liquid Flame]]
  * **Sorrowmoss Seed** → [[en:rpd:potion_of_paralytic_gas_item|Potion of Paralytic Gas]] or [[en:rpd:potion_of_invisibility_item|Potion of Invisibility]] (random)
  * **Dreamweed Seed** → [[en:rpd:potion_of_mind_vision_item|Potion of Mind Vision]]
  * **Earthroot Seed** / **Fadeleaf Seed** → [[en:rpd:potion_of_toxic_gas_item|Potion of Toxic Gas]]
  * **Icecap Seed** → [[en:rpd:potion_of_frost_item|Potion of Frost]]
  * **Rotberry Seed** → [[en:rpd:potion_of_purity_item|Potion of Purity]] (no alchemyClass, random treasury pick)
  * **Moongrace Seed** → Random potion (no alchemyClass defined)

==== Arrow Creation ====
  * Specialized arrows can be created by combining regular arrows with potions:
    * [[en:rpd:fire_arrow_item|Fire Arrow]] - From arrows and [[en:rpd:potion_of_liquid_flame_item|Potion of Liquid Flame]]
    * [[en:rpd:health_arrow_item|Health Arrow]] - From arrows and [[en:rpd:potion_of_healing_item|Potion of Healing]]
    * [[en:rpd:paralysis_arrow_item|Paralysis Arrow]] - From arrows and [[en:rpd:potion_of_paralytic_gas_item|Potion of Paralytic Gas]]
    * [[en:rpd:poison_arrow_item|Poison Arrow]] - From arrows and [[en:rpd:potion_of_toxic_gas_item|Potion of Toxic Gas]]

==== Alchemy Ingredients ====
  * [[en:rpd:seed_item|Seeds]] - Primary ingredient, obtained from plants
  * Water - Implicit ingredient (not an item) for most potion brewing

==== Strategy ====
  * Collect multiple seeds of the same type to increase chance of desired potion
  * Sungrass seeds are most valuable for reliable healing potions
  * Throwing mixed seeds gives weighted random results
  * The alchemy pot in the Sewers is the only fixed location; some levels may have additional pots
