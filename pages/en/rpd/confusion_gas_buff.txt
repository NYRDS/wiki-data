====== Confusion Gas ======

{{ rpd:images:vertigo_buff.png|Confusion Gas }}

**Confusion Gas** is a gas cloud (blob) that can be created by [[en:rpd:rotten_food_item|rotten food]] when consumed or thrown.

==== Effects ====
  * Applies the [[en:rpd:vertigo_buff|Vertigo]] effect to all creatures in the area of effect
  * Gas volume: 150 + 10 * dungeon depth (from rotten food) or 300 + 20 * dungeon depth (from Dreamweed)
  * Vertigo duration: target's resistance factor × 10 turns

==== Obtaining ====
  * 25% chance when consuming or throwing [[en:rpd:rotten_food_item|rotten food]]
  * Also generated by Dreamweed plants (300 + 20 * dungeon depth gas volume)

==== Mechanics ====
  * The gas is created when rotten food spoils (see rotten_food.lua)
  * On each gas evolution tick, applies Vertigo to all characters in the area
  * Vertigo duration depends on target's resistance factor (durationFactor × 10)
  * Uses Speck.CONFUSION for visual particles (purple particles)

==== Strategy ====
  * Avoid consuming rotten food to prevent generating the gas
  * When throwing rotten food, consider the chance of generating gas
  * Use the gas deliberately to affect enemies
  * Heroes with Ring of Elements (Vertigo resistance) receive reduced duration
