====== Ice Caves Level ======

**Ice Caves** is a special dungeon level in Remixed Dungeon that features cold-themed enemies and mechanics.

==== Description ====
The Ice Caves level is an underground area filled with ice-themed challenges and enemies. This level features unique mechanics related to freezing effects and cold-based creatures. It uses custom tilesets with icy walls, chasms, and frozen water.

==== Features ====
  * **Environment**: An icy underground cave system with freezing effects, chasms, and ice vein visual effects
  * **Enemies**: Cold-themed creatures like Kobolds, Kobold Icemancers, Ice Elementals, and Cold Spirits
  * **Hazards**: Ice traps, freezing areas from Icecap plants, and chasms
  * **Special Mechanics**: Temperature-based effects and ice-related mechanics

==== Enemies ====
  * [[en:rpd:kobold_mob|Kobolds]] - Common enemies in Ice Caves
  * [[en:rpd:kobold_icemancer_mob|Kobold Icemancers]] - Kobolds with ice magic
  * [[en:rpd:ice_elemental_mob|Ice Elementals]] - Powerful icy constructs (spawn deeper in the level)
  * [[en:rpd:cold_spirit_mob|Cold Spirits]] - Fast flying spirits immune to frost
  * **Boss**: [[en:rpd:ice_guardian_core_mob|Ice Guardian Core]] - Boss of the Ice Caves

==== Items ====
  * [[en:rpd:ice_key_item|Ice Keys]] for unlocking frozen areas
  * [[en:rpd:frozen_carpaccio_item|Frozen Carpaccio]] - Dropped by Ice Elementals and Ice Guardians, provides random beneficial effects when eaten
  * [[en:rpd:icecap_seed_item|Icecap Seeds]] - Can be found in treasury rooms

==== Special Mechanics ====
  * **Freezing Effects**: Icecap plants freeze adjacent cells; Freezing blob spreads on flammable terrain
  * **Chasms**: Ice Caves feature chasms between unconnected rooms
  * **Ice Veins**: Visual ice crystal effects on wall decorations
  * **Caged Kobold Quest**: Special quest encounter at depth 18
