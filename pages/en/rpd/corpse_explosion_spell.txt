====== Corpse Explosion ======

==== Description ====
**Corpse Explosion** is a Plague Doctor affinity spell that consumes a corpse (Carcass item) on the target cell to create a toxic gas cloud and spread multiple harmful gases to adjacent cells. The power of the explosion scales with the corpse's value and the caster's skill level.

==== Mechanics ====
  * **Magic Affinity**: PlagueDoctor
  * **Targeting Type**: Cell — must target a cell containing a Carcass item
  * **Spell Level**: 3
  * **Cast Time**: 2 turns
  * **Mana Cost**: 9
  * **Cooldown**: 10 turns

==== Effects ====
When cast on a cell containing a Carcass:
  * **Corpse Consumption**: The Carcass item is removed from the heap
  * **Base Power**: Corpse's price (gold value) × 6 = corpse health value
  * **Gas Strength**: floor(corpseHealth × 0.5 × casterSkillLevel)
  * **Central Cell**: Creates a **Toxic Gas** cloud at the target cell with the full gas strength
  * **Adjacent Cells**: Spreads three gas types cyclically to orthogonal adjacent cells (up/down/left/right):
    * **Paralytic Gas** — strength = floor(gasStrength × 0.3)
    * **Confusion Gas** — strength = floor(gasStrength × 0.3)
    * **Miasma Gas** — strength = floor(gasStrength × 0.3)
  * **Visuals**: Shadow particles (CURSE type) burst at the target cell
  * **Sound**: Cursed sound effect
  * **Failure Message**: "CorpseExplosion_NoCorpse" if no Carcass is present on the target cell

==== Requirements ====
  * Target cell must contain a **Carcass** item (any mob corpse with "Carcass" prefix)
  * Cannot be cast on empty cells or cells with non-carcass items

==== Strategy ====
  * High mana cost (9) and long cooldown (10 turns) limit spam usage
  * Most effective with high-value corpses (bosses, high-level mobs) and high skill levels
  * The gas spread to adjacent cells provides area denial and crowd control
  * Paralytic Gas prevents enemy movement/actions, Confusion Gas causes friendly fire, Miasma Gas deals damage over time
  * Synergizes with Plague Doctor's corpse-generating mechanics and Carcass-related items
  * Consider positioning to catch multiple enemies in the gas spread
