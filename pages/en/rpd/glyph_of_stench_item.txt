====== Glyph of Stench ======

The Glyph of Stench is an [[en:rpd:armor_glyphs|armor glyph]] that can appear on certain types of armor in Remixed Dungeon. This glyph releases toxic gas around the attacker when the wearer is hit.

==== Description ====
When an enemy hits the wearer of armor with this glyph, there is a chance that a cloud of toxic gas will be released at the attacker's position, poisoning them and potentially other nearby enemies.

==== Mechanics ====
Grounded in [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/scripts/glyphs/Stench.lua|Stench.lua]]:
  * **Activation:** Triggers on adjacent hits, chance (armor level + 5) >= 4
  * **Effect:** Places a ToxicGas blob at the attacker's position with volume 20
  * **Area of Effect:** The gas spreads and affects all creatures in the area
  * **Scaling:** Higher armor levels increase activation chance

==== Armor Compatibility ====
  * Can appear on any armor as a random glyph inscription

==== Strategy ====
  * Excellent for crowd control - gas affects multiple enemies
  * Can poison the wearer if they stay in the gas cloud
  * Effective against groups of melee enemies
  * Combine with gas immunity for safe usage

==== Identification ====
  * Armor with this glyph appears as normal until activated
  * Can be identified through various means like [[en:rpd:scroll_of_identify_item|Scroll of Identify]]

==== Acquisition ====
  * [[en:rpd:armor_item|Armor]] randomly generated with this glyph
  * [[en:rpd:scroll_of_enchantment_item|Scroll of Enchantment]]
  * [[en:rpd:artificer_npc|Artificer NPC]] services
