====== Spider Queen ======

{{ rpd:images:spider_queen_mob.png|Spider Queen }}

==== Description ====
**Spider Queen** is a boss-level mob in Remixed Dungeon. She is the matriarch of the spider creatures found in the Spider Level.

==== Stats ====
  * **HP**: 120
  * **HT**: 120 (source: [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/RemixedDungeon/src/main/assets/mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json#L13|mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json:L13]])
  * **Defense Skill**: 18 (source: [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/RemixedDungeon/src/main/assets/mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json#L8|mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json:L8]])
  * **Attack Skill**: 21 (source: [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/RemixedDungeon/src/main/assets/mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json#L7|mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json:L7]])
  * **Damage**: 12-20 (source: [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/RemixedDungeon/src/main/assets/mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json#L9-L10|mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json:L9-L10]])
  * **DR (Damage Resistance)**: 10 (source: [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/RemixedDungeon/src/main/assets/mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json#L11|mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json:L11]])
  * **EXP for Kill**: 11 (source: [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/RemixedDungeon/src/main/assets/mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json#L14|mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json:L14]])

==== Abilities ====
  * **Spider summoning**: Randomly spawns Spider Eggs during combat (approximately every 21 turns) (source: [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/RemixedDungeon/src/main/assets/scripts/mobs/SpiderQueen.lua#L20-L24|scripts/mobs/SpiderQueen.lua:L20-L24]])
  * **Poison attack**: Has a 50% chance to apply poison to enemies on hit (source: [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/RemixedDungeon/src/main/assets/scripts/mobs/SpiderQueen.lua#L27-L31|scripts/mobs/SpiderQueen.lua:L27-L31]])
  * **Fire immunity**: Immune to fire-based attacks and burning effects (source: [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/RemixedDungeon/src/main/assets/mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json#L24|mobsDesc/SpiderQueen.json:L24]])
  * **Retreat behavior**: When below 50% HP, attempts to stay at range during combat (kite behavior) (source: [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/RemixedDungeon/src/main/assets/scripts/mobs/SpiderQueen.lua#L35-L38|scripts/mobs/SpiderQueen.lua:L35-L38]])

==== Special Mechanics ====
  * **Spawn Loot**: On first spawn, randomly receives a Chaos Crystal, Spider Charm, or Spider Armor (source: [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/RemixedDungeon/src/main/assets/scripts/mobs/SpiderQueen.lua#L40-L54|scripts/mobs/SpiderQueen.lua:L40-L54]])
  * **Boss Validation**: Validates SPIDER_QUEEN_SLAIN badge on death (source: [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/RemixedDungeon/src/main/assets/scripts/mobs/SpiderQueen.lua#L57-L59|scripts/mobs/SpiderQueen.lua:L57-L59]])

==== Drops ====
  * **[[en:rpd:spider_queen_carapace_item|Spider Queen Carapace]]** - A valuable crafting material
  * Chaos Crystal, Spider Charm, or Spider Armor (random drop, received on spawn)
  * Skeleton Key (guaranteed drop on kill)

==== Location ====
  * Found in the Spider Level as the boss of that area
