====== Rat King's Crown ======

{{ rpd:images:rat_king_crown_item.png|Rat King's Crown }}

==== Description ====
The **Rat King's Crown** is a unique artifact in Remixed Dungeon. It is the golden crown of "His Ratness" and provides the wearer with the ability to tame rats they defeat in combat.

According to the game: "The golden crown of His Ratness, symbol of everrating Power"

==== Properties ====
  * **Type:** [[en:rpd:artifacts|Artifact]]
  * **Effect:** When attacking a rat while wearing this crown, the rat becomes a friendly pet
  * **Buff:** Grants the **Rat King Aura** buff ([[en:rpd:rat_king_aura_buff|Rat King Aura]]) when equipped
  * **Identification:** Always identified (cannot be unidentified)
  * **Sprite:** Artifacts atlas at index 17

==== Mechanics ====
  * The crown provides the **Rat King Aura** buff when equipped
  * When the player attacks a [[en:rpd:rat_mob|Rat]] while the buff is active, the rat is tamed via `Mob.makePet()` and becomes an ally
  * Only affects regular rats, not rat bosses or special variants
  * The buff icon is `BuffIndicator.RATTNESS`
  * Buff name: "Ratness"
  * Buff description: "Hail the Lord of the rats, every rat shall obey and praise."

==== Strategy ====
  * Extremely valuable in areas with high rat populations (Sewers, Prison)
  * Can build a small army of pet rats over time
  * Works well with the [[en:rpd:pet_mechanic|Pet Mechanic]] for additional allies
  * The crown is automatically identified when found

==== Obtaining ====
  * Dropped by the [[en:rpd:rat_king_mob|Rat King]] boss
  * Part of the Rat King quest line
