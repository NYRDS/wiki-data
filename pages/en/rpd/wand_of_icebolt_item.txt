====== Wand of Icebolt ======

{{ rpd:images:wand_of_icebolt_item.png|Wand of Icebolt }}

The **Wand of Icebolt** is an ice-based wand that fires bolts of freezing energy, dealing frost damage to targets and applying freezing effects.

==== Item Overview ====
  * **Type:** Wand
  * **Rarity:** Uncommon
  * **Special Effect:** Deals ice damage and applies Frost and Slow debuffs
  * **Damage Type:** Magic (ice/frost)

==== Special Mechanics ====
  * **Ice Damage:** Deals direct ice-based damage to targets (3+level to 4+level*2)
  * **Frost Application:** Applies Frost buff for (duration factor * 2.5 + level) duration
  * **Slow Application:** Applies Slow buff for (duration factor * 5 + level) duration
  * **Line of Sight:** Icebolts travel in a straight line to the target
  * **Charges:** Standard wand charges, can be increased with upgrades
  * **Identification:** Always known (isKnown() returns true)
  * **Visual Effects:** Emits snow particles when equipped

==== Strategy ====
  * Effective against fire-based enemies and enemies vulnerable to cold
  * The Frost debuff reduces enemy damage output and can freeze them
  * The Slow debuff reduces enemy speed, making kiting easier
  * Can be used to create distance between you and pursuing enemies
  * Particularly useful against fast enemies that rely on melee attacks
  * Avoid using on cold-resistant enemies

==== Obtaining ====
  * Found in Ice Caves levels
  * Available in shops at moderate prices
  * Dropped by ice-themed enemies
  * Found in containers and special rooms in cold areas

==== Effect ====
When fired, the Wand of Icebolt shoots a bolt of ice at the targeted enemy, dealing ice damage and applying both Frost and Slow debuffs.

According to the game's string resources: "This wand launches an ice bolt, dealing light damage to the target. There is also a chance to slow down or even freeze the enemy."
