====== Deferred Damage ======

{{ rpd:images:defered_damage_buff.png|Deferred Damage }}

**Deferred Damage** is a buff in Remixed Dungeon that stores incoming damage and deals it back to the character over time.

==== Description ====
When a character has the Viscosity glyph on their armor, there is a chance that incoming damage will be deferred instead of being taken immediately. The damage is stored in the Deferred Damage buff and then dealt back at a rate of 1 damage per turn until the stored damage is depleted.

According to the string resources: "The pain has been turned into a slowing pain, lessening the damage taken at once. Damage left to receive %d"

==== Mechanics ====
  * **Source:** Viscosity glyph on armor
  * **Trigger Chance:** Based on armor level - `Random.Int(level + 7) >= 6`
  * **Damage Storage:** All deferred damage accumulates in the buff's damage field
  * **Damage Release:** 1 damage per turn (TICK) until depleted
  * **Buff Icon:** BuffIndicator.DEFERRED
  * **Visual:** Purple glow on armor (Viscosity glyph)

==== Behavior ====
  * When damage is deferred, the character takes 0 immediate damage
  * The deferred damage is added to the buff's damage counter
  * Each turn, the buff deals 1 damage to the character
  * If the character dies from deferred damage, it counts as a glyph death
  * The buff detaches when stored damage reaches 0 or character dies

==== Stats ====
  * **Duration:** Variable (depends on accumulated damage)
  * **Damage per Turn:** 1
  * **Stacks:** Yes (damage accumulates)
  * **Removable:** No (only by depleting damage or death)
