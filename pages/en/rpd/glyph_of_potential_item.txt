====== Glyph of Potential ======

The Glyph of Potential is an [[en:rpd:armor_glyphs|armor glyph]] that can appear on certain types of armor in Remixed Dungeon. This glyph discharges electricity through both the attacker and defender when hit.

==== Description ====
When an enemy hits the wearer of armor with this glyph, there is a chance that electricity will arc between the attacker and defender, damaging both parties.

==== Mechanics ====
Grounded in [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/scripts/glyphs/Potential.lua|Potential.lua]]:
  * **Activation:** Triggers on adjacent hits, chance (armor level + 7) >= 6
  * **Damage to Attacker:** Random 1 to original damage amount, as electricity damage
  * **Damage to Defender:** Random 1 to attacker's damage amount, as electricity damage
  * **Visual:** Lightning bolt effect between attacker and defender, camera shake for hero
  * **Scaling:** Higher armor levels increase activation chance

==== Armor Compatibility ====
  * Can appear on any armor as a random glyph inscription

==== Strategy ====
  * Punishes enemies for attacking, especially high-damage enemies
  * Self-damage can be risky - consider health pool before relying on it
  * Effective against enemies with low electricity resistance
  * Camera shake provides visual feedback on activation

==== Identification ====
  * Armor with this glyph appears as normal until activated
  * Can be identified through various means like [[en:rpd:scroll_of_identify_item|Scroll of Identify]]

==== Acquisition ====
  * [[en:rpd:armor_item|Armor]] randomly generated with this glyph
  * [[en:rpd:scroll_of_enchantment_item|Scroll of Enchantment]]
  * [[en:rpd:artificer_npc|Artificer NPC]] services
