====== Glyph of Displacement ======

The Glyph of Displacement is an [[en:rpd:armor_glyphs|armor glyph]] that can appear on certain types of armor in Remixed Dungeon. This glyph provides teleportation-based defensive abilities to the wearer.

==== Description ====
When an enemy hits the wearer of armor with this glyph, there is a chance that the wearer will be teleported to a random visible, passable location on the current level, escaping immediate danger.

==== Mechanics ====
Grounded in [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/scripts/glyphs/Displacement.lua|Displacement.lua]]:
  * **Activation:** Triggers when the wearer is hit by an enemy
  * **Probability:** Number of teleport attempts = (armor level + 1) * 5 (minimum 1 if armor is cursed)
  * **Effect:** Teleports the wearer to a random visible, passable cell with no other character
  * **Limitations:** Does not activate on boss levels

==== Armor Compatibility ====
  * Can appear on any armor as a random glyph inscription

==== Strategy ====
  * Can be a lifesaver when cornered or surrounded
  * Random teleportation may move the hero to a worse position
  * Does not work on boss levels - plan accordingly
  * Combines well with high evasion builds

==== Identification ====
  * Armor with this glyph appears as normal until activated
  * Can be identified through various means like [[en:rpd:scroll_of_identify_item|Scroll of Identify]]

==== Acquisition ====
  * [[en:rpd:armor_item|Armor]] randomly generated with this glyph
  * [[en:rpd:scroll_of_enchantment_item|Scroll of Enchantment]]
  * [[en:rpd:artificer_npc|Artificer NPC]] services
