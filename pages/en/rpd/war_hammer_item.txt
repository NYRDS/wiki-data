====== War Hammer ======
{{ rpd:images:war_hammer_item.png|War Hammer }}

The War Hammer is a type of [[en:rpd:melee_weapons|melee weapon]] in Remixed Dungeon that focuses on dealing heavy damage with slow, powerful strikes.

==== Description ====
The War Hammer is a heavy, blunt weapon designed for dealing massive damage to enemies. It sacrifices speed for devastating impact power, making it a high-damage melee option.

==== Weapon Properties ====
  * **Damage Type:** Physical melee damage
  * **Speed:** Slow attack speed (1.2f delay factor)
  * **Accuracy:** Average accuracy (1f)
  * **Upgrade Potential:** Can be upgraded with [[en:rpd:scroll_of_upgrade_item|Scrolls of Upgrade]]
  * **Enchantments:** Compatible with [[en:rpd:weapon_enchantments_mechanic|weapon enchantments]]
  * **High Damage:** Significant damage output per hit (tier 5 weapon)

==== Stats ====
  * **Damage:** 5–36 (base min 5, base max 36 at tier 5)
  * **Speed:** 1.2f delay factor (relatively slow)
  * **Accuracy:** 1f accuracy factor
  * **Strength Requirement:** 18 (typical for tier 5 weapons)
  * **Durability:** Standard melee weapon durability

==== Mechanics ====
  * **Heavy Hitter:** High base damage range (5–36) trades speed for power
  * **Slow but Powerful:** 1.2f delay factor means careful positioning is essential
  * **Strength Dependent:** Damage scales with hero's strength; requires 18 STR for no penalty
  * **Heavy Attack:** Uses HEAVY_ATTACK animation class for special combat animations
  * **Upgrade Scaling:** Each upgrade increases min damage by 1 and max damage by tier (5)

==== Obtaining ====
  * War hammers can be found throughout the dungeon
  * May be available in shops, depending on the level
  * Relatively rare due to high power level
