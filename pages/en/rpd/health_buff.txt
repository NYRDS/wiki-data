====== Health ======

{{ rpd:images:health_buff.png|Health }}

**Health** is a buff in Remixed Dungeon that provides regenerative healing while the hero remains stationary on the cell where the buff was applied.

==== Effect ====
  * **Regeneration**: Heals max(1, max HP / 10) HP per turn while the hero stays on the same cell
  * **Duration**: Persists until the hero moves off the cell or reaches full HP
  * **Visual**: Shaft particle effect at the cell location

==== Sources ====
  * **Sungrass Plant**: Stepping on a [[en:rpd:sungrass_level_object|Sungrass]] plant applies this buff
  * **Warden Subclass**: Heroes with the [[en:rpd:warden_subclass|Warden]] subclass have a chance to gain this buff when interacting with plants

==== Mechanics ====
  * The buff checks the hero's position each turn
  * If the hero moves to a different cell, the buff immediately detaches
  * If the hero's HP reaches maximum, the buff detaches
  * Healing amount scales with max HP (1/10 of max HP, minimum 1)
  * Uses 5 time units per healing tick (STEP)

==== Strategy ====
  * Stand on Sungrass plants to recover health safely
  * Useful for conserving potions and food
  * Combine with [[en:rpd:warden_subclass|Warden]] subclass for more frequent plant buffs
  * The buff is lost if you move, so plan your positioning carefully
