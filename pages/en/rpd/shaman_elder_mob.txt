====== Shaman Elder Mob ======

{{ rpd:images:shaman_elder_mob.png|Shaman Elder Sprite }}

==== Description ====
The **Shaman Elder** is a special variant of the Gnoll Shaman mob found in the dungeon. This particular gnoll looks much older than other shamans and surely has a higher status in the tribe. A strange aura slowly forms around him, which seems to reflect all that touches it.

==== Stats ====
  * **Level**: Appears in levels 13-14 (very rare in level 13, more common in level 14)
  * **HP**: 48
  * **EXP for killing**: 9
  * **Defense**: 4 (Damage Reduction)
  * **Attack Skill**: 20
  * **Defense Skill**: 14
  * **Damage**: 8-12
  * **Attack Delay**: 1.0 seconds
  * **Attack Range**: 6 tiles (ranged)
  * **Movement Speed**: Normal (1.0x)

==== Special Abilities ====
  * **Kiting AI**: Maintains distance from enemies (kiteMinDist = 2)
    - When enemy is within 1-2 tiles, the Shaman Elder will flee
    - When enemy is more than 4 tiles away, it will hunt them down
  * **Mana Shield**: When hit by a magical attack (zap), activates a Mana Shield buff at skill level
  * **Loot**: Always drops a Spell Book (100% chance)

==== Behavior ====
The Shaman Elder uses a "Kite" AI behavior, preferring to attack from range and maintain distance. It will flee when enemies get too close (within 2 tiles) and pursue when they are far away.
