====== Hooked Dagger ======

{{ rpd:images:hooked_dagger_item.png|Hooked Dagger }}

**Hooked Dagger** is a Lua-scripted melee weapon in Remixed Dungeon. It is a dagger-type weapon that causes bleeding on hit.

==== Description ====
The Hooked Dagger is a specialized dagger with a hooked blade designed to tear at flesh. When it strikes an enemy, it applies a bleeding effect that deals damage over time.

==== Stats ====
  * **Type:** Melee Weapon (Dagger)
  * **Slot:** Weapon slot
  * **Image:** Item index 3 from `items/daggers.png` sprite sheet
  * **Price:** 17 gold
  * **Required STR:** 9
  * **Attack Delay Factor:** 1.0
  * **Accuracy Factor:** 1.0
  * **Damage:** Level to Level×2 (scales with item level)

==== Mechanics ====
  * **Bleeding Proc:** On successful hit, applies the [[en:rpd:bleeding_buff|Bleeding]] buff to the target for 3 turns
  * **Level Scaling:** Damage scales with item level (1-2 damage per level)
  * **Dual-wielding Interaction:** Equips in main weapon slot, blocks left hand slot
  * **Visual Name:** Displays as "Wand" (may be a placeholder)
  * **Attack Animation:** Uses staff animation class

==== Acquisition ====
  * Available through standard item generation (shops, loot, etc.)
  * Can be found in the dungeon as random loot

==== Usage Tips ====
  * Excellent for applying consistent damage over time through bleeding
  * Bleeding effect stacks with multiple hits
  * Low STR requirement (9) makes it accessible to most builds
  * Damage scaling makes it viable through mid-game

==== Strategy ====
The Hooked Dagger shines in extended fights where the bleeding damage accumulates. Its low strength requirement makes it a good early-game weapon for characters who haven't invested heavily in strength. The bleeding proc is particularly effective against high-HP enemies where the damage-over-time represents a significant portion of their health pool.
