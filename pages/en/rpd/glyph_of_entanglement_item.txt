====== Glyph of Entanglement ======

The Glyph of Entanglement is an [[en:rpd:armor_glyphs|armor glyph]] that can appear on certain types of armor in Remixed Dungeon. This glyph provides root and armor-buffing defensive abilities to the wearer.

==== Description ====
When an enemy hits the wearer of armor with this glyph, there is a chance that the wearer will become rooted in place while gaining a temporary armor buff, trading mobility for protection.

==== Mechanics ====
Grounded in [[https://github.com/NYRDS/remixed-dungeon/blob/master/scripts/glyphs/Entanglement.lua|Entanglement.lua]]:
  * **Activation:** 25% chance when the wearer is hit by an enemy
  * **Effect:** Applies Roots buff for 5 - floor(armor level / 5) turns
  * **Armor Buff:** Raises an Armor pool buff to level 5 * (armor level + 1)
  * **Visual:** Shows earth particles and camera shake on activation
  * **Trade-off:** Roots prevent movement but provide significant damage reduction

==== Armor Compatibility ====
  * Can appear on any armor as a random glyph inscription

==== Strategy ====
  * Powerful against heavy hitters - the armor buff reduces incoming damage significantly
  * Dangerous in situations requiring mobility (chasm rooms, trap avoidance)
  * Works well with ranged combat where rooting is less penalizing
  * The armor buff persists after the root effect ends

==== Identification ====
  * Armor with this glyph appears as normal until activated
  * Can be identified through various means like [[en:rpd:scroll_of_identify_item|Scroll of Identify]]

==== Acquisition ====
  * [[en:rpd:armor_item|Armor]] randomly generated with this glyph
  * [[en:rpd:scroll_of_enchantment_item|Scroll of Enchantment]]
