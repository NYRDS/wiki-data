====== Earthroot Plant ======

{{ rpd:images:earthroot_level_object.png|Earthroot Plant }}

==== Description ====
The **Earthroot Plant** is a defensive plant that grows from an [[en:rpd:earthroot_seed_item|Earthroot Seed]]. When stepped on, it applies an **Earthroot Armor** buff (buff ID "Armor") based on the pressing character's max HP.

==== Mechanics ====
  * Grows from a planted [[en:rpd:earthroot_seed_item|Earthroot Seed]]
  * When pressed, grants **Earthroot Armor** with level equal to the character's max HP (ht())
  * Earthroot Armor absorbs damage into a pool while the hero stays on the same cell; moving away removes the buff
  * Visual effect: earth particles and camera shake
  * Implemented in Lua: `scripts/plants/Earthroot.lua`
  * Buff implementation: `scripts/buffs/Armor.lua`

==== Note ====
This plant grants **Earthroot Armor**, not [[en:rpd:barkskin_buff|Barkskin]]. The two buffs are different:
  * **Barkskin** provides Damage Reduction (DR) that decays by 1 per turn
  * **Earthroot Armor** provides a damage absorption pool that depletes as damage is taken, but only while stationary
