====== Earthroot Plant ======

{{ rpd:images:earthroot_plant.png|Earthroot Plant }}

==== Description ====
The **Earthroot Plant** is a defensive plant that grows from an [[en:rpd:earthroot_seed_item|Earthroot Seed]]. When stepped on, it applies an [[en:rpd:barkskin_buff|Barkskin]] armor buff based on the pressing character's health.

==== Mechanics ====
  * Grows from a planted [[en:rpd:earthroot_seed_item|Earthroot Seed]]
  * When pressed, grants [[en:rpd:barkskin_buff|Barkskin]] armor equal to 1/4 of the character's health
  * Visual effect: earth particles and camera shake
  * Implemented in Lua: `scripts/plants/Earthroot.lua`
