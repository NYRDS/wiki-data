====== Dialogue ======

**Dialogue** in Remixed Dungeon is the primary system for interacting with NPCs (Non-Player Characters). Through dialogue, players can receive quests, purchase items, obtain services, and gather information about the dungeon.

==== How Dialogue Works ====

When the player approaches an NPC and uses the interaction action (typically by tapping/clicking on the NPC), a dialogue window opens presenting options based on the NPC's role and the player's current state.

==== Dialogue Types ====

==== Shop Dialogue ====
  * **[[en:rpd:shopkeeper_npc|Shopkeeper]]** - Buy and sell items
  * **[[en:rpd:wandmaker_npc|Wandmaker]]** - Specialized wand vendor
  * **[[en:rpd:imp_npc|Imp]]** - Quest-related shop after completing tasks

==== Quest Dialogue ====
  * **[[en:rpd:ghost_npc|Ghost]]** - Offers quests (kill Fetid Rat, find Dried Rose)
  * **[[en:rpd:imp_npc|Imp]]** - Offers quests (kill Golems or Monks)

==== Service Dialogue ====
  * **[[en:rpd:healer_npc|Healer NPC]]** - Free healing and hunger restoration
  * **[[en:rpd:shopkeeper_npc|Shopkeeper]]** - Item identification services (in some versions)

==== Dialogue Options ====
Typical dialogue options include:
  * **Trade/Shop** - Open buying/selling interface
  * **Talk/Listen** - Receive hints, lore, or quest information
  * **Quest** - Accept, progress, or turn in quests
  * **Service** - Use NPC's special service (healing, identification, etc.)
  * **Leave** - Close dialogue

==== Quest-Specific Dialogue ====
NPCs with quests have dynamic dialogue that changes based on quest state:
  * **Initial** - Quest offer with description and reward
  * **In Progress** - Encouragement or status check
  * **Complete** - Reward delivery and closure
  * **Post-Quest** - May offer additional services or lore

==== Technical Implementation ====
Dialogue is implemented through:
  * **Java Classes**: NPC behavior in `com/nyrds/pixeldungeon/actors/mobs/npc/`
  * **Lua Scripts**: Custom dialogue logic in `scripts/npc/`
  * **String Resources**: All dialogue text in `values/strings_all.xml`
