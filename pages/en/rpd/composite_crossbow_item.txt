====== Composite Crossbow ======

{{ rpd:images:composite_crossbow_item.png|Composite Crossbow }}

**Composite Crossbow** is a tier-4 ranged weapon, a reinforced version of the wooden crossbow.

==== Description ====
This reinforced crossbow proves to be a more efficient tool compared to its wooden counterpart. It offers improved accuracy and damage that scale with weapon level.

==== Stats ====
  * **Tier:** 4
  * **Min Damage Factor:** 1.1
  * **Max Damage Factor:** 1.6
  * **Accuracy Formula:** 1 + level * 0.5
  * **Damage Formula:** 1 + level * 0.75
  * **Delay Factor:** 1.1
  * **Sprite Index:** 3 (crossbow sprite sheet)
