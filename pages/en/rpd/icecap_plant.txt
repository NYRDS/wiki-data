====== Icecap Plant ======

{{ rpd:images:icecap_plant.png|Icecap Plant }}

==== Description ====
**Icecap** is one of the magical [[en:rpd:plants|plants]] in Remixed Dungeon that can be grown from its seed ([[en:rpd:icecap_seed_item|Icecap Seed]]). When activated, it releases a cloud of pollen that freezes nearby creatures.

In-game description: "Upon touching an Icecap excretes a pollen, which freezes everything in its vicinity." (''Icecap_Desc'', strings_all.xml#L1103)

==== Stats ====
  * **Seed:** [[en:rpd:icecap_seed_item|Icecap Seed]] (item kind `Icecap.Seed`)
  * **Appearance:** Small blue flower (sprite frame index 1, per `plantsDesc/Icecap.json`)
  * **Effect Range:** The plant cell plus all 8 adjacent cells
  * **Primary Effect:** [[en:rpd:frost_buff|Freeze]] on every non-LOS-blocking cell in range
  * **Alchemy Class:** Potion of Frost (''IcecapSeed.java'' sets `alchemyClass = PotionOfFrost.class`)

==== Effects ====
  * Releases the freezing effect when any character steps on it
  * Affects the source cell and all eight neighbours; cells blocked by line of sight are skipped
  * The effect is identical to [[en:rpd:potion_of_frost_item|Potion of Frost]]
  * When eaten: heals and applies a doubled [[en:rpd:frost_buff|Frost]] buff (duration `5 x durationFactor x 2`)

==== Mechanics ====
  * Found growing naturally in the dungeon, including in the Treasury and Spiders Treasury levels
  * Grown by planting [[en:rpd:icecap_seed_item|Icecap Seeds]]
  * Activates when any character (hero or enemy) steps on it
  * Effect area: 1 tile radius (immediate adjacent tiles, excluding LOS-blocked cells)
  * When eaten: heals `Random.Int(0, max((maxHP - currentHP) / 4, 10))` and applies a 2x duration Frost buff

==== Strategy ====
  * Excellent for freezing groups of enemies in tight spaces
  * Useful against fast-moving enemies that are difficult to hit
  * Can be eaten for emergency healing and a long Frost buff
  * Consider planting in corridors to freeze enemies before combat
  * LOS-blocking terrain (walls, boulders) shields adjacent cells from the freeze
