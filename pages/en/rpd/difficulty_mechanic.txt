====== Difficulty ======

**Difficulty** in Remixed Dungeon determines the overall challenge level of the game, affecting enemy stats, experience requirements, hero starting conditions, and various game mechanics.

==== Difficulty Levels ====

The game has 4 difficulty levels (0-3):

  * **Easy (0)**: Reduced enemy stats, lower EXP requirements, more generous starting conditions
  * **Normal (1)**: Standard difficulty
  * **Hard (2)**: Increased enemy stats and spawn rates
  * **Expert (3)**: Maximum challenge, additional restrictions

==== Effects of Difficulty ====

=== Experience Requirements ===
  * **Easy (0)**: 5 + (current level × 4) EXP per level
  * **Normal/Hard/Expert (>0)**: 5 + (current level × 5) EXP per level

=== Hero Starting Stats ===
  * **Easy/Normal**: 30 base HP
  * **Hard/Expert**: 20 base HP

=== Enemy Scaling ===
  * Mob HP, damage, and accuracy scale with difficulty
  * Boss mechanics change (e.g., Lich spawns 2/3/4 Runic Skulls on Easy/Normal/Hard+)
  * Yog spawns 2 mini-bosses on Easy/Normal, 3 on Expert

=== Economy ===
  * Shop prices and service costs scale with difficulty factor
  * Healing services at NPCs cost more on higher difficulties
  * Identification costs increase with difficulty

=== Special Mechanics ===
  * **Hunger**: Hero does not suffer hunger debuffs on difficulties allowing save states while on safe levels
  * **Save States**: Easy/Normal allow save/load; Expert may restrict this
  * **Badges**: Some badges require winning on specific difficulties
  * **Challenges**: Additional challenge modifiers can be enabled on top of base difficulty

==== Difficulty Factor ====

The game uses a difficulty factor (via `GameLoop.getDifficultyFactor()`) that multiplies various costs and scales enemy stats. This factor is:
  * Easy: ~0.75x
  * Normal: 1.0x
  * Hard: ~1.25x
  * Expert: ~1.5x

==== Code References ====

  * **Hero.java**: Starting HP, EXP formula (`expToLevel()`), difficulty-specific mechanics
  * **GameLoop.java**: `getDifficulty()`, `getDifficultyFactor()` methods
  * **Dungeon.java**: Hero creation with difficulty parameter
  * **Level.java**: Mob spawning and scaling via Bestiary
  * **Bestiary.json**: Mob spawn weights per level (difficulty affects final spawn rates)
