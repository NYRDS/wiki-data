====== Fire Enchantment ======

**Fire Enchantment** is a [[en:rpd:enchantments|weapon enchantment]] in Remixed Dungeon that has a chance to set enemies on fire when hitting them.

==== Description ====
Fire Enchantment has a chance to ignite enemies on hit. This causes additional damage over time from the [[en:rpd:burning_buff|Burning]] effect.

==== Mechanics ====
  * **Chance to ignite enemy:** **Random.Int(weapon_level + 3) >= 2**
    * Level 0: 33% chance
    * Level 1: 50% chance
    * Level 2: 60% chance
    * Higher levels: increasing chance
  * On proc, applies [[en:rpd:burning_buff|Burning]] effect for several turns
  * Additional fire damage: random value from 1 to (weapon level + 2)
  * Emits flame particles: Level + 1 particles
  * Fire damage stacks with existing weapon fire damage bonus
  * Visually glows orange (color: 0xFF4400)
  * Effect applied through proc() method with probability increasing with weapon level

==== Strategy ====
  * Effective against enemies vulnerable to fire
  * Works well with fast weapons for frequent procs
  * Damage over time helps in prolonged fights
  * Useful against enemies that fear fire
